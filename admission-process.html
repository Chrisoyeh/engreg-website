Admission Process

"Follow these simple steps to enrol your child online, or visit our school office."

Step-by-Step Admission Guide

1

Purchase the Form

Go to Apply Online, click "Get Form", and complete your purchase online. Once payment is confirmed, the admission form is opened for you. You can also visit the school office.

For online applications, payment must be completed successfully before the admission form is displayed.
2

Fill & Submit

Complete the admission form with all the required information, then click Submit.

3

Upload Documents

Upload all the required documents, then click Submit to complete your application.

4

Application Successful

A success message is sent once your application has been completed successfully.

Check the Admission Update page for entrance examination dates, successful candidates, and the admission list.

Complete your admission application online quickly, securely, and conveniently from anywhere.
