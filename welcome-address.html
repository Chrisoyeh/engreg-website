A Welcome Message From The Supervisor

Welcome to Nazareth School, where faith, discipline, and academic excellence come together to nurture every child.

Dear Parents, Students, and Visitors,

It is with great joy and gratitude that I welcome you to Nazareth School. Our school is built on a strong foundation of faith, discipline, and academic excellence. We are committed to nurturing every child entrusted to our care — developing not only their intellect but also their character and moral values.

At Nazareth School, we believe that every child is gifted and capable of greatness. Our dedicated team of educators work tirelessly to create a supportive and inspiring environment where students can thrive and reach their full potential.

God bless you all.

Sr. Irene Ugwoegbu

Supervisor, Nazareth School
