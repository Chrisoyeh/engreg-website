Sports & Physical Development

Our football field, basketball court, and indoor sports facilities give pupils room to play, compete, and stay active. Sport teaches discipline, teamwork, and a healthy, active lifestyle.

How Sports Benefit Our Pupils:
  • Build Discipline: Focus, commitment, and good sportsmanship.
  • Develop Teamwork: Learning to cooperate and support one another.
  • Healthy Lifestyle: Active habits that last a lifetime.
