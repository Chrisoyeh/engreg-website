Welcome Address from the Head of School

Dear Parents, Guardians, and Friends of Engreg School A,

It is my absolute privilege and joy to welcome you to our School A section. Here, we lay the critical foundations upon which all future learning, discovery, and character development are built.

Our commitment to quality education is at the core of everything we do. We offer a child-centred learning experience that recognizes the unique potential, talents, and pacing of every young mind. By fostering a warm, homely, and exciting environment, we help children transition smoothly from early exploration to structured primary learning.

At Engreg, we believe that academic excellence must walk hand-in-hand with character development. We nurture moral values, creative expression, self-confidence, and physical wellness in our pupils. Our dedicated and loving teaching team employs modern educational practices and active learning aids to make schooling a delightful experience.

We deeply value our active partnership with parents. We believe that by working closely together, we can provide a supportive community that helps our children flourish academically, socially, and morally. We invite you to explore our curriculum and discover how we inspire a lifelong love for learning in our children.

Ms. Nnenna Ukagba

Head of School

Head Teacher, School A Section
